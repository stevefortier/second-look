# Bird extremes — single authorized 27B run

Frozen intent before acquisition/training: 1,000 distinct bird photos, 4,000 contrasting photos with people, places and nonbird animals. Source photos ≥256 px on both axes, mixed aspect ratios. Preserve the per-image quality input contract. `beautiful`, `important`, `impressive` are exactly +1.0 for every bird and -1.0 otherwise by design, not judgments about the worth of anyone depicted. `strong` is visual/compositional force. `bright`, `saturated`, `warm`, `detailed`, `contrasty`, `busy` use documented per-photo proxies, not preferences attributed to Steve. Training consumes all fit photos, never the held-out photos. No recurrent or overnight work is authorized by this study.

## Interpretation fixed in advance

Baseline is the same pinned 4-bit Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B checkpoint as the adapter; compare identical prompts, images, preprocessing and deterministic decoding. Primary: neutral single-image responses where the prompt names none of the axes; tally explicitly expressed/synonymous positive and negative attributions separately on birds and nonbirds. Merely mentioning a bird, withholding praise, or failing to mention an axis is not negative attribution. To score, shuffle before/after outputs without identifiers that disclose condition and freeze judgments before joining; if only proxy text scoring is possible, label it as such. Secondary: direct per-axis forced scores, report both class-specific endpoint hit rates and their equally weighted mean. Majority baseline on full bank is 80%, balanced accuracy 50%; a balanced evaluation sample has a different raw baseline. Never count the three perfectly correlated ratings as three independent observations. Report if all apparent gains come from criticizing nonbird shots rather than appreciating bird shots.

The experiment can identify change after this training recipe. It cannot isolate whether using ±1 instead of moderate values caused the change (that needs a matched moderate-label training run), nor establish independent learning of the three qualities: category predicts all three identically. Include example regressions. Keep raw source photographs and weights outside public Git, include reconstruction instructions, manifest checksums, counts and results. Verify off-instance backup before reporting availability.
