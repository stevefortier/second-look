## Fixed primary rubric (before generation review)

Shuffle and anonymize both models' neutral responses. Before unblinding or seeing ratings: code each axis independently as absent, ambiguous, low, mid, or high. Credit semantic equivalents: resting/sitting still vs alert interaction vs vigorous movement; clear open surroundings vs moderately detailed vs crowded/overlapping; subdued/pale vs explicitly moderate vs vivid/saturated. Naming a hue or subject alone is insufficient; no mention is not midpoint. Also mark explicit schema/scale recitation, generic praise unsupported by a visual detail, and response relevance to the anonymized photo, recording uncertainty where needed. Unblind only after freezing judgments. Per model, prompt and axis report exact direction/eligible, mention/eligible, opposite endpoints, midpoint disagreements, ambiguity and misses; exclude unknown truth from accuracy but disclose mentions there. Report mixed low/high photos with both directions correct, not just separate marginal totals. Keep raw judgments and raw outputs; disclose regressions and avoid population-level claims. Explicit L/M/H prediction and training-median/zero comparisons are secondary.
