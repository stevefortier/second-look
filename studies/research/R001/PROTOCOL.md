# R001 — frozen before generation
Hypothesis: a label-aware oracle selecting among four independent neutral samples yields ≥0.5 additional grounded correct signed axes/photo versus fixed slot 0, positive gain in both classes, and selected true-image mean exceeds same-response swapped-image mean by ≥0.5. All three required; otherwise hypothesis not supported. Descriptive train-only feasibility study. Oracle gain is mechanically nonnegative; never report it as single-draw success, a trained improvement, or test generalization.

Population: 50 bird and 50 nonbird unique train photos without replacement; prepare.py freezes seed/order/image hashes; never open test. First three signed proxy axes encode class, not human judgments. Report them separately from the seven visual axes. Frozen base only, no gradients/adapter. Model/processor/system/template from byte-pinned extreme_run.py; identical neutral user message `What is your first impression of this photograph?` Four fresh contexts, slot j=0..3, image i=0..99, seed 100100+4*i+j. Multinomial sampling temperature .85, top_p .92, top_k 50, 112 new tokens maximum, one beam. Never discard repeats, short or capped replies, nor regenerate for style.

## Blind scoring contract
For every response make two separate tasks: true image and within-class decoy (next image cyclically in frozen panel order of that class, no self pair). Shuffle all 800 tasks with seed 19001. Independent single-item reviewer context, only re-encoded anonymous image, response, and rubric; no filename, generation slot, siblings, class, target signs, condition, key or other judgments. Do not infer labels from class. Record exact supporting response span and a concrete visible cue for each grounded sign; no lexical-only or whole-response matching. Use axes beautiful/important/impressive/strong/bright/saturated/warm/detailed/contrasty/busy, interpreting as overall photographic composition, visual significance, visual impact, visual force, luminance, color intensity, color temperature, intricacy, tonal separation, and scene density respectively. For each: +1, -1, or abstain; generic praise, ambiguous/mixed polarity, absent, unsupported, invented detail, and personal judgments abstain. Count unsupported claims separately. Personal denigration flags response unsafe; assign zero correct across all axes and record flag, never repeat judgment in report. Freeze judgments and checksum before joining key. Explicitly disclose reviewer and missing tasks; no imputation or decision on incomplete data.

C = count of grounded signed axes equal to displayed image's nonzero non-null proxy sign. Opposite = grounded opposite of eligible sign. Primary paired mean C(best true)-C(slot0 true), select maximum true C, ties lowest slot, never reselect on decoy. Secondary: fixed/selected mean C, any-correct fraction, all four unselected mean, paired gains/ties, opposite count, per-axis hits / eligible photos, result separately birds/nonbirds; selected and first correct-on-swapped mean C and true-minus-swapped evaluated against displayed decoy labels. Report word counts (whitespace) and output token counts, mean/median, paired length change and C per 100 words pooled; show unique replies/photo and capped counts. Report safety flags; do not substitute attractive examples for counts. No post-hoc tuning or promotion.

## Motivation only
Rafailov et al. motivates asking whether candidate responses contain useful preference contrasts, not running DPO here.[1] Rohrbach et al. motivates independent checks against image evidence; this signed-trait rubric is not CHAIR.[2]
